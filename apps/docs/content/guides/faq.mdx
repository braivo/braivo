## Classes and learners

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Can we publish a course to one class only?">

No. Every learner who has joined your school reaches every published course, whichever groups they are in, and **Hide from learners** hides a course from all of them. Groups decide whose progress a teacher follows, not who practises: see [who sees what](./follow-progress.mdx).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who sees a learner’s progress?">

The learner, your school's Owners and Admins, and the teachers of the learner's groups; preparing courses shows no one's progress. [Who sees what, and what it means](./follow-progress.mdx).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a learner see why an answer is right?">

Yes. After each answer they see the right one, the question's explanation if it has one, and the source passages it quotes. After a miss, **Review this topic** can show what that part of your material says. If a question seems wrong, **Report a problem** sends your school the question, the answer, and a note. [Check explanations and passages before publishing](./review-and-publish.mdx#check-each-question).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How much do learners practise at a time?">

Practice comes in sessions of ten answers. Each day a learner starts at most twenty new objectives in your school; what is due for review, or still being learned, is never held back. Braivo chooses what comes next: a miss comes back soon, and a right answer returns before the learner is likely to forget it. [What each standing means](./follow-progress.mdx#the-four-standings).

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Languages

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Which languages do learners see Braivo in?">

The learning site and its sign-in email are in English or Polish, following the learner's browser language; the learning site has no language menu yet. A school can't yet set one language for all its learners whatever their browsers ask for. The console, mostly in English past signing in, has its own language menu, whose choice doesn't carry over to the learning site. [What is translated](https://github.com/braivo/braivo/blob/main/docs/specs/localization.md).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a course be in another language?">

Yes. Questions and explanations can be in the language your learners need, while the passages they quote stay in the source's language. Braivo's own draft writes for the learners you describe. Braivo has no one-click translation of a course: ask your AI app to prepare the other-language version, then review it before publishing. [What to ask your AI app for](./draft-with-ai-app.mdx#what-to-ask-for).

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Your material and AI

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Do learners get our PDF?">

No. Only your school's Owners, Admins, and Authors can download a file you added, and it downloads rather than opening in the page. Learners see the passages a question quotes, after they answer it. To give them the whole reading, share it as you do now. [Add your material](./first-course.mdx#add-your-material).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are learners’ practice answers sent to an AI?">

No. Braivo grades its multiple-choice and typed questions without an AI model, and records the results to decide what each learner practises next. Connecting an AI app to prepare courses doesn't give it learner lists or progress reports. [What teachers see of those results](./follow-progress.mdx).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does our material go to an AI?">

Not unless you use one: the console reads a PDF's own text without AI. Braivo's own AI, if whoever runs Braivo for you turns it on, sends what it reads or drafts from to Anthropic, the company that provides its model. An AI app you connect sends what it reads to the company that provides it, under its terms: [check that your school allows that](./draft-with-ai-app.mdx).

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Learners' data

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="What happens to a learner’s data when they leave?">

An Owner or Admin removing a learner, in the settings' **Members**, ends their access and keeps their practice history. To export one learner's data from your school, or erase their history there, keeping or ending their membership, your school's designated contact arranges it with whoever runs Braivo. A whole-account export or erasure, across every school, needs the account holder's verified request and instructions from every school whose records it includes. [What whoever runs Braivo does](./deployment.mdx#learners-data).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does erasure remove every copy?">

Erasing a learner's history removes their recorded practice. It doesn't redact names written into teaching material you uploaded. Whoever runs Braivo, restoring an older backup, must apply the erasure again before reopening it: ask them how they keep backups, and for how long. [Erasing one school's history](./deployment.mdx#erasing-one-schools-history).

</Accordion>

</Accordions>
