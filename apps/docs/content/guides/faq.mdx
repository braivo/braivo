## Classes and learners

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Can we publish a course to one class only?">

No. Every learner who has joined your school reaches every published course, whichever groups they are in, and **Hide from learners** hides a course from all of them. Groups decide whose progress a teacher follows, not who practises: see [who sees what](./follow-progress.mdx).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who sees a learner’s progress?">

The learner sees their own. Your school's Owners and Admins see every learner's, and a group's teacher sees the learners of the groups they teach, in the courses those groups take. Preparing courses shows no one's progress: an Author who teaches no group sees none. [Who sees what, and what it means](./follow-progress.mdx).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a learner see why an answer is right?">

Yes. After each answer they see the right one, the question's explanation if it has one, and the passages of your material it quotes. After a miss, **Review this topic** can show what that part of your material says. If a question seems wrong, **Report a problem** sends your school the question, the answer, and a note. [Check explanations and passages before publishing](./review-and-publish.mdx#check-each-question).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How much do learners practise at a time?">

Practice comes in sessions of ten answers. Each day a learner starts at most twenty new objectives in your school; what is due for review, or still being learned, is never held back. Braivo chooses what comes next: a miss comes back soon, and a right answer returns before the learner is likely to forget it. [What each standing means](./follow-progress.mdx#the-four-standings).

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Languages

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Which languages do learners see Braivo in?">

The learning site, signing in, and the sign-in email are in English or Polish: Braivo follows the learner's browser, and a learner can choose from a menu. A school can't yet set one language for all its learners whatever their browsers ask for. The console, past signing in, is mostly in English. [What is translated](https://github.com/braivo/braivo/blob/main/docs/specs/localization.md).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a course be in another language?">

Yes: a course is in the language of your material and of the questions written from it. Braivo's own draft writes for the learners you describe, and your AI app writes in the language you ask for. Braivo doesn't translate a course, and questions quote your material, so the same course in a second language needs material in that language. [What to ask your AI app for](./draft-with-ai-app.mdx#what-to-ask-for).

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Your material and AI

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Do learners get our PDF?">

No. Only your school's Owners, Admins, and Authors can download a file you added, and it downloads rather than opening in the page. Learners see the passages a question quotes, after they answer it. To give them the whole reading, share it as you do now. [Add your material](./first-course.mdx#add-your-material).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does our material go to an AI?">

Not unless you use one: the console reads a PDF's own text without AI. Braivo's own AI, if whoever runs Braivo for you turns it on, sends what it reads or drafts from to Anthropic, the company that provides its model. An AI app you connect sends what it reads to the company that provides it, under its terms: [check that your school allows that](./draft-with-ai-app.mdx).

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Learners' data

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="What happens to a learner’s data when they leave?">

An Owner or Admin removing a member, in the settings' **Members**, ends their access and keeps their history. Your school can instruct whoever runs Braivo to export one learner's data from your school, or erase their history there, keeping or ending their membership. Exporting or erasing a whole account, across every school, takes the account holder's own request. [How it is done](./deployment.mdx#learners-data).

</Accordion>

</Accordions>
