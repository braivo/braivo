Braivo turns existing educational content into adaptive practice, under its owner's brand. Each learner spends their time on what they need to learn or remember next, instead of following the same fixed sequence as everyone else.

## Where to start

- [Authoring a course from existing material](../guides/authoring.mdx), in the console, with your own AI app, or with your own scripts.
- [Use Braivo from your AI app](../guides/assistants.mdx), such as Claude or ChatGPT.
- [The API, end to end](../guides/api.mdx), to build Braivo into your own product.
- [Deployment](../guides/deployment.mdx), to run Braivo yourself.
