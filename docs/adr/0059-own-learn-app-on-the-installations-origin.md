# 0059: Braivo's own learn app on the installation's origin, at `/learn` and `/c/<course ID>`

Status: accepted (2026-11-08); amends [ADR 0004](0004-one-application-origin.md) and [ADR 0011](0011-ui-and-auth-client-packages.md)

## Context

A person's own learning, material they keep for themselves ([ADR 0046](0046-material-in-steps.md)), needs an address of Braivo's, not a school's. ADR 0004 gives the root of the installation's origin to the console and each school's learn app to its own domains, where a course is `/c/<number>-<label>`, its number unique within its organization alone ([ADR 0044](0044-course-numbers.md)). ADR 0046 keeps that material in an organization of the person's own, but nothing tells such an organization from a school: owning one says nothing, as a school's owner owns it too.

## Decision

- **The installation's origin gives Braivo's own learn app three route families, `/learn`, `/learn/…`, and `/c/…`**, whole, whatever page each holds. Its pages are `/learn`, My learning, the person's own home, and `/c/<course ID>`, one of their own courses by its ID: `braivo.app/learn` and `braivo.app/c/<course ID>` on Braivo Cloud. Under `/learn/…` are its files, `/learn/assets/`, apart from the console's `/assets/`, and any page it adds later. Everything else stays the console's: `/`, `/login`, `/join`, `/<slug>/…`, `/consent`, `/device`, `/invitations/…`, and `/api` the server's. `learn` is a reserved slug already; `c` joins it, as does every top-level route the learn app has. `/c/…` on the origin is conditional on `c`: before the router sends it to the learn app, the installation checks that no organization holds the slug, and if one does, ADR 0004's rule decides (the route takes another name or the organization moves) before anything ships. `learn.braivo.app` stays reserved, at most a redirect here later.
- **One build serves school domains and the origin.** On a school's domain it is unchanged: `/` the school's home, `/c/<number>-<label>` its courses. There, `/learn` redirects to `/` on the same domain: My learning is the origin's alone, and the school's home is what that address means on its domain. On the origin, `/learn` is My learning's canonical address. What the host serves, an organization or the installation itself, comes from the server (`GET /api/host`), never from the path, so a school's domain stays the school's whatever path it is reached at.
- **The router in front sends `/learn`, `/learn/…`, and `/c/…` to the learn app**, as it sends hosts (ADR 0004): Cloud's Worker on Braivo Cloud, a self-hoster's proxy elsewhere ([deployment](../../apps/docs/content/guides/deployment.mdx)). In development, the console's dev server proxies them to the learn app's.
- **An apex course link names the same course for everyone: its ID.** `braivo.app/c/<course ID>` is one course, whoever opens it; the server then checks the viewer may read it, and anyone else gets it unavailable. A number never resolves on the origin. The course ID is what the learn app already links on a host serving no organization, unique and stable, and needs nothing new; a label may follow it later, as on school domains.
- **The origin opens personal courses only.** A school's course opens on its domain, `https://<learn domain>/c/<number>-<label>`, under its brand, and My learning links it there. The learn app on the origin opens a course only from those the server lists as in a personal organization of the viewer's. The API on the installation's host stays as it is, as integrators practise through it ([API guide](../../apps/docs/content/guides/api.mdx)): the limit is where the learn app goes, not what the account may read.
- **An organization is personal by whose it is, recorded at creation** ([ADR 0060](0060-personal-organization-is-one-accounts.md)): never inferred from anyone's role in it. Only personal organizations are My learning's; only the others count as schools wherever the origin chooses where someone goes. Existing organizations are schools, and nothing reclassifies one. An account may own a school and a personal organization, made in either order, and sets the latter up explicitly; visiting `/learn` creates neither.
- **It takes no address a school would want.** No learn domain, then or later, as its courses are at the installation's `/c/<course ID>` and a domain would serve them under a brand as a school's: claiming, activating, and the operator's registration refuse a personal organization before storing anything or asking the provisioner, and the console offers it none. Its slug is `p-` and 16 random letters (80 bits), naming it in the console and in no address anyone chose: a readable slug would claim a word from schools' namespace for each person, and a slug is required wherever organizations are. Named after the account, its email while it has none, as a name the person recognizes in the console; renamed as any organization is.
- **The account's session, as it is.** The origin holds it (ADR 0018), so no handoff and no second sign-in. Signed out, `/learn` and `/c/…` go to the console's `/login`, which brings them back to the exact page, loaded, as the learn app is not the console's to route.
- **A bare `/` resumes the last-used area.** The browser remembers, per account, the last of My learning and the organizations whose console they opened; `/learn` records My learning, `/<slug>` that organization. The console's `/` reads it once the page has loaded and goes there while it is still theirs. Missing or stale, someone with staff access to a school (`owner`, `admin`, `author`, or a group's teacher) gets its console, the one last opened here, else the first by name; everyone else, learners and those with no school or only a personal organization, gets My learning, never `/join`, which stays where a school is set up. A preference that grants nothing, read only at bare `/`: every other address reaches its own page.
- **My learning lists the person's own courses**, saying so while there are none and then leading to the site of each school they belong to, through its sign-in, and leads back to the console of each school they work in. Opening it creates nothing. Whether it lists their schools' courses too, each linking to its domain, is open.
- **Braivo's colours on Braivo's own host** (amending ADR 0011): the learn app wears an organization's name on its domain and Braivo's on the installation's origin, never Braivo's on a school's domain. Until it does, it is unbranded there, as on any host serving no organization.
- **Uploaded material is never rendered as markup or script on the origin.** The learn app's scripts run with the console's origin, so a script slipped in through material — a document's HTML, a PDF renderer's flaw — would act with the account's session, a teacher's or an owner's permissions included. A path prefix isolates nothing here. The app shows what material holds as text and Braivo's own components, under the same Content-Security-Policy as the console (`script-src 'self'`, [ADR 0049](0049-app-security-headers.md)); rendering a file's own markup, or running anything it carries, needs a sandboxed origin of its own first. If third-party scripts or interactive uploaded content become central to the learn app, a host of its own is reconsidered.

## Alternatives rejected

- **`learn.braivo.app`.** Isolates the learn app's scripts from the account's session, at the price of a handoff or a second session for every account, teacher and learner alike, and a `/login` per host. That isolation matters once the app runs what it does not control; until then the origin's session is the simpler whole.
- **Everything under `/learn`, a course at `/learn/c/<course>`.** A course would have two shapes of address, one per kind of host, and every apex link a segment longer.
- **`/c/<number>` within the viewer's own organization.** The same address would open Alice's course for Alice and Bob's for Bob: a shared or bookmarked link would silently name another course.
- **A slug the person chooses, or one from their name.** A word taken from schools for each person, chosen for a page no learner sees.
- **`/learn` on a school's domain as a second My learning**, or unanswered. The person's own material would appear under a school's brand, or a guessed address would fail.
- **Limiting the installation's host to personal organizations**, a ceiling as on school domains (white-label-4). Integrators practise through that host's API; the learn app's limit is its own.
- **Bare `/` always the console, or always My learning.** One of a teacher and a learner gets a page that is not theirs at every visit. Remembering the last area costs `/` its stability as a bookmark: it lands where the browser last was, so `/learn` and `/<slug>` are the addresses to keep.
- **Choosing `/` in Cloud's Worker.** It would need a cookie or a preference stored on the server, for what one browser remembers.
- **The learn app's pages inside the console**, or **a build per host.** Two apps' routes and branding in one bundle, or two artifacts kept alike for one difference.

## Consequences

- A teacher's `braivo.app/` returns to the console they last used, a learner's to My learning; the console no longer sends a learner from `/` to their school's domain.
- Bookmarks are `/learn`, `/<slug>`, and `/c/<course ID>`; `/` follows the browser.
- Every top-level path of the learn app is held from slugs, so a new one is reserved before it ships.
- Done in this repository: `c` and `learn` reserved; a personal organization marked at creation, as whose it is (`personal_owner_id`, ADR 0060); `GET /api/host`; the learn app at `/learn` and `/c/<course ID>`, its files under `/learn/assets/`, the dev proxy, and sign-in through the console's `/login`; My learning, and a school's domain sending `/learn` to its own `/`; bare `/` resuming the last-used area; setup counting the schools an account owns alone; setting up a personal organization through the API.
- Not built yet: a way to set up a personal organization in the apps, so the API is it for now, and authoring there is the console's, at its slug, as ADR 0046's gap says; Braivo's colours on the origin.
