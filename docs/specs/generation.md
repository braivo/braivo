# Generation

Status: living; checked against the code on 2026-10-04.

A content owner without an AI app of their own turns their material into a course with the installation's own AI: Braivo reads a PDF or photo into pages, drafts objectives and tasks from a source, and the owner reviews the draft in the console before authoring what they keep ([product.md](../product.md), core jobs 1 and 4). A model proposes and Braivo checks: everything a draft offers quotes passages located in the source ("Grounded AI"), and nothing is stored until the owner accepts it, as ordinary objectives, citations, tasks, and a course.

## Rules

- **generation-1:** Reading a file answers its words page by page, each page labelled with its printed number (or its place from 1), in reading order, pages without words left out, in the shape a paged source takes. Nothing is stored: adding the pages as a source is the caller's next request. `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/pdf-to-tutor.test.ts`
- **generation-2:** Braivo reads a PDF of at most 24 MB, or a PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP image of at most 7.5 MB; a larger one is a 400 saying how to send less, and any other file a 400 saying to extract its text another way. An installation that keeps no files answers 501; a file the organization does not have, 404. `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`
- **generation-3:** Drafting from a source answers objectives in teaching order, each with its citations and tasks, multiple-choice or typed, in the shapes the objective, citation, and task endpoints take, plus `refused`, the list of what the model proposed and Braivo left out. The model is asked for typed tasks where recall matters, vocabulary above all, a word or meaning per objective, and for one kind per objective; Braivo enforces only that each task is valid. A malformed optional field of a task is read as absent, refusing the task only if it is then invalid, never the draft; an answer language Braivo cannot read is left out. Nothing is stored. `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/pdf-to-tutor.test.ts`
- **generation-4:** A draft keeps only what traces to the source: a quote located exactly once in it, a task valid by the task endpoint's rules with at least one located quote, and an objective with a valid title, at least one located quote, and at least one kept task. Everything else is refused by name and reason, such as "Objective “Greetings”, task “¿Hola?”: the quote “Hola, amigo.” does not occur in the source.": the owner reviews only what was kept, so the model's positions would point nowhere. The objective's title, the task's question, and the quote's words each have their whitespace collapsed and are cut to 80 code points. A hand-written task may cite nothing; a drafted one may not. `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`
- **generation-5:** A draft holds at most twelve objectives and five tasks each, and only the first three quotes of a task and five of an objective are looked for; whatever is past a cap is named in `refused`. So one draft, accepted whole, fits the authoring endpoints' 200 quotes per request. `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`
- **generation-6:** A source over 200,000 characters is a 400 saying to add it as several sources, a chapter each. The audience, the owner's description of the learners, is optional and at most 200 characters, else a 400. `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`
- **generation-7:** Each drafted objective's key is random, made with the draft: accepting the same draft again finds the objectives it already made, and another draft of the same source makes new ones. `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/pdf-to-tutor.test.ts`
- **generation-8:** Only an `owner`, `admin`, or `author` of the organization may read a file or draft with the installation's AI, under its gate and quota, which an author does not manage ([ADR 0048](../adr/0048-author-role.md)); anyone else gets a bare 403, before anything about the installation's model, the organization's entitlement, or its sources is revealed. `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/roles.test.ts`
- **generation-9:** Without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, both requests answer 501 with `code` `AI_UNAVAILABLE` and an `error` pointing to one's own AI app connected to Braivo; reading a file where the installation keeps no files answers generation-2's uncoded 501 first. With a key, every organization may use it unless `BRAIVO_AI_ORGANIZATIONS` lists the IDs that may; any other answers 403 with `code` `AI_NOT_ENTITLED` and an `error` saying to ask the operator. That variable set but naming no ID, or `*`, stops the server at startup, as does a key without it on an installation with `BRAIVO_SELF_SERVE_DOMAIN` (access-31). `BRAIVO_AI_MODEL` picks the model, `claude-sonnet-5` by default. `apps/server/config.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`
- **generation-10:** Every request that reaches the model is recorded for its organization: what was asked (`read` or `draft`), by whom, and when. A request refused as invalid, unknown, or because the stored file could not be fetched is not recorded; one the model then fails still is. `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`
- **generation-11:** With `BRAIVO_AI_MONTHLY_LIMIT` set, a whole number of at least 1, an organization that has made that many requests in the calendar month (UTC) is answered 429, with `Retry-After` until the first of next month and an `error` naming that date and one's own AI app meanwhile. Two requests racing for the last one cannot both have it. Unset, requests are recorded and never capped. `apps/server/persistence/ai-request.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/server/config.test.ts`
- **generation-12:** A model that cannot be reached, answers an error, is cut short, or answers in another shape, or a file in which it finds no words, is a 502 with an `error` saying which; nothing is stored. `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`
- **generation-13:** Reading and drafting wait for the model past the server's ten-second idle timeout, bounded by the model's own five-minute one. A caller that goes away stops Braivo waiting and asks the provider to stop; the request still counts (generation-10). While a draft or a reading runs, the console says it can take a few minutes; it aborts a draft, an upload, and a reading when the owner leaves the page, and when a proxy in front of Braivo cuts a draft or a reading off (504, or Cloudflare's 524, without Braivo's reason) it says so, that the request may have counted, and to send less material or have the proxy wait longer, rather than to try again. `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/console/test/sources.test.tsx`, `apps/console/test/drafting.test.tsx`
- **generation-14:** In the console, adding material with a file and no pasted text has Braivo read the file, where its AI is on (generation-19), and adds its pages as a source with the file as its original. A file outside the types and sizes Braivo reads (generation-2) is refused before it is uploaded, saying what to do instead; with text pasted, a file the upload takes is kept as the original, unread. A refused addition sent again does not read the same file twice. `apps/console/test/sources.test.tsx`
- **generation-15:** The console reviews a draft on its source's page, and only there: what Braivo refused is listed, every objective and task is kept until unticked, dropping an objective drops its tasks, and nothing is stored until the owner creates the course. Leaving for another page while a draft is under review asks first, and so does reloading or closing the tab where the browser allows; once creating the course has failed, the question and the review say that some or all of it may already be stored, which neither leaving nor discarding undoes. `apps/console/test/drafting.test.tsx`
- **generation-16:** In review, an objective's title can be corrected, and a task's question and explanation, a choice task's options and correct option, and a typed task's accepted answers, one a line (its answer language is kept); a task's number of options cannot be changed, and neither an objective's passages nor a task's can be edited, since Braivo located them in the source. A task's edit is checked as the server would (blank, repeated, too long, too many accepted answers, not text) before it is saved, and the course cannot be created while an edit is open. `packages/ui/compositions/task-editor.test.tsx`, `apps/console/test/drafting.test.tsx`
- **generation-17:** In review, creating the course checks its title and its kept objectives' titles first (present, at most 500 characters, text), and that each kept objective keeps a task, since learners would wait on one without any (learner-loop-5); it sends nothing until they pass, saying what is wrong under the first field to fix and focusing it. Once sent, the reviewed fields are locked: trying again after a failure sends exactly what was first sent, so a retry that succeeds finishes that course rather than duplicating it, and Braivo's reason for a refusal is shown. Creating gives up when its four requests in a row, under one deadline, take longer than 10 seconds each plus the time what is sent takes at 100 KB/s, and a sign-out meanwhile keeps the review, offering sign-in in a new tab before trying again. Discarding remains possible after a failed attempt. `apps/console/test/drafting.test.tsx`, `apps/server/api/client.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/pdf-to-tutor.test.ts`
- **generation-18:** Accepting a draft is authoring through the ordinary endpoints, as the person accepting: objectives by their keys, then citations, then tasks, then a course keyed by the source, its title, and its objectives' keys. The same accepted draft again resolves to the same course, and keeping a different set of objectives makes another; keeping different tasks under the same objectives resolves to the same course, those objectives keeping every task added to them. A course accepted from a draft is ordinary authored content, and the learner loop does not know AI wrote it. `apps/server/api/pdf-to-tutor.test.ts`
- **generation-19:** Listing the organizations someone works in says, for each they author in, whether they may use the installation's AI: a model set and the organization let (generation-9), whatever its month's requests (generation-11); the entry of one where they only teach omits it (generation-8). Where it is off, the console says so before anything is prepared (on Courses with none yet, above Add material, and in place of a source's draft form), pointing to whoever runs Braivo or one's own AI app, such as Claude or ChatGPT, and refuses a file with no pasted text before uploading, asking for its text. Wherever the console offers one's own AI app, the offer links to the guide connecting one, in a new tab. A draft already asked for stays, under way or under review, and if it fails says why there. A refusal coded `AI_UNAVAILABLE` or `AI_NOT_ENTITLED` despite the listing is worded as its AI being off, never naming the operator's settings; any other, such as storage gone, keeps Braivo's words. `apps/server/application/permission.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/client.contract.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/console/test/courses.test.tsx`, `apps/console/test/sources.test.tsx`, `apps/console/test/drafting.test.tsx`

## Boundaries

- What a source is, its pages, original file, immutability, and how a quote is located and cited: [sources](sources.md).
- Objectives, courses, tasks, their keys and idempotence, and the MCP tools an AI app drafts with: [authoring](authoring.md). Drafting with one's own AI app through `/mcp` is the alternative to this area, at the owner's own AI cost, and needs no key on the installation.
- Who holds `owner`, `admin`, or `author`, and sign-in: [access](access.md).
- Accepting a draft in the console publishes its course as it is created, the console being where a person reviews; a course or task a tool writes through the authoring API waits for publishing there ([ADR 0039](../adr/0039-published-in-the-console.md)).
- Not here yet: AI grading and feedback on a learner's answer ([learner loop](learner-loop.md) until it outgrows it), a vocabulary lesson of more than twelve words in one draft (each word is an objective), video and audio, which reach Braivo as captions.

## Decisions

- [ADR 0021](../adr/0021-citations.md): proposals are not stored; review happens where a draft is made.
- [ADR 0024](../adr/0024-idempotent-authoring.md): keyed writes, so accepting a draft again finishes it rather than duplicating it.
- [ADR 0029](../adr/0029-server-drafting.md): Braivo drafts on request with one model port, checks the draft against the source, and stores nothing.
- [ADR 0030](../adr/0030-server-extraction.md): Braivo reads a PDF or photo into pages, returned for the caller to add; one gate for every AI request.
- [ADR 0031](../adr/0031-ai-limits.md): each AI request is recorded, and an operator may cap an organization's month.
- [ADR 0048](../adr/0048-author-role.md): an `author` reads files and drafts under the organization's gate and quota, managing neither.
- [ADR 0046](../adr/0046-material-in-steps.md), accepted, not built yet: extraction and drafting in the background, a PDF's text layer before any model, a draft with a goal and a scope.
- [ADR 0047](../adr/0047-structured-items.md), accepted, not built yet: structured items converted by Braivo beside drafting.

## Gaps

| Gap                                                                                                               | Impact                                                                                                                                      | Next step                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reading and drafting are synchronous, tens of seconds for a chapter                                               | A proxy with a shorter timeout in front of Braivo cuts them off, still counted against the quota; the owner waits on the page               | Extractions and drafts as background jobs, a step at a time ([ADR 0046](../adr/0046-material-in-steps.md))                               |
| No stored draft                                                                                                   | Leaving, once confirmed or where the browser cannot ask (generation-15), loses a review, and a new draft costs a request                    | Stored with drafting as a job (ADR 0046)                                                                                                 |
| No record of the model or prompt that produced a draft or reading                                                 | An owner or operator cannot tell which version wrote a course, or compare versions                                                          | Record the model and a prompt version on each `ai_request` row                                                                           |
| The quota counts requests, not cost                                                                               | A 200,000-character chapter costs the operator many times a photo, under the same count                                                     | Meter tokens where cost matters (Braivo Cloud does downstream); a per-organization limit later                                           |
| One limit for every organization, and nothing shows the ledger                                                    | An operator cannot give one school more, or see usage without querying the database                                                         | A per-organization setting and a console page, when a self-hoster needs them                                                             |
| A redraft's objectives are new, never matched to earlier ones                                                     | Drafting the same source again and accepting it duplicates objectives already authored                                                      | Match a redraft to existing objectives when teachers need it                                                                             |
| A transcription can misread a smudged scan or handwriting, skip a page, or lose a question's number or answer key | Quotes and tasks inherit the misreading; the owner sees it only on the source's page (sources-20), after adding the pages                   | An extraction the owner can correct before saving it as a source (ADR 0046); meanwhile add the material with pasted text                 |
| A PDF over 24 MB, or longer than one answer holds, cannot be read                                                 | A question bank of hundreds of pages is refused; a book is read a chapter at a time, by hand                                                | Read in the background, a batch of pages at a time (ADR 0046)                                                                            |
| Every page goes to the model, even where the PDF's own text layer has its words                                   | Credits are spent on text a parser reads without a model; an organization without Braivo's AI cannot read a born-digital PDF in the console | Extract the text layer first, the model only for pages the owner chooses (ADR 0046)                                                      |
| A draft is of a whole source, told only the audience                                                              | A long book must be split into sources to be drafted; the owner cannot say what learners should be able to do, or which pages matter        | A goal and a scope of the source (ADR 0046)                                                                                              |
| Drafting writes new tasks; it does not convert questions a source already holds                                   | A question bank drafted is rewritten, partly, instead of kept with its answer keys                                                          | A converter by the owner's agent ([authoring guide](../guides/authoring.md)); Braivo's own ([ADR 0047](../adr/0047-structured-items.md)) |
| One provider, Anthropic                                                                                           | An operator with another provider or their own endpoint cannot turn on Braivo's AI                                                          | Another small adapter behind the model port                                                                                              |
