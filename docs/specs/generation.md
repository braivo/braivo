# Generation

Status: living; checked against the code on 2026-11-07.

A content owner without an AI app of their own turns their material into a course with the installation's own AI: Braivo reads a PDF or photo into pages, drafts objectives and tasks from a source, and the owner reviews the draft in the console before authoring what they keep ([product.md](../product.md), core jobs 1 and 4). A model proposes and Braivo checks: everything a draft offers quotes passages located in the source ("Grounded AI"), and nothing is stored until the owner accepts it, as ordinary objectives, citations, tasks, and a course.

## Rules

- **generation-1:** Reading a file answers its words page by page, each page labelled with its printed number (or its place from 1), in reading order, pages without words left out, in the shape a paged source takes. Nothing is stored: adding the pages as a source is the caller's next request. `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/pdf-to-tutor.test.ts`
- **generation-2:** Braivo reads a PDF of at most 24 MB, or a PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP image of at most 7.5 MB; a larger one is a 400 saying how to send less, and any other file a 400 saying to extract its text another way. An installation that keeps no files answers 501; a file the organization does not have, 404. `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`
- **generation-3:** Drafting from a source answers objectives in teaching order, each with its citations and tasks, multiple-choice or typed, in the shapes the objective, citation, and task endpoints take, plus `refused`, the list of what the model proposed and Braivo left out. The model is asked for typed tasks where recall matters, vocabulary above all, a word or meaning per objective, and for one kind per objective; Braivo enforces only that each task is valid. A malformed optional field of a task is read as absent, refusing the task only if it is then invalid, never the draft; an answer language Braivo cannot read is left out. The owner's goal, audience, and language for learners' instructions reach the model as delimited, labelled blocks of data describing the purpose and the learners, never instructions, their `<`, `>`, and `&` escaped, as in the source's title, so that none can forge a block, and it is told that objectives and tasks come only from the source: an instruction, not a guarantee, since generation-4 proves that quotes exist, not that a task serves the goal, which the person reviewing decides. The model's answer states first, as `unsupported`, what of the goal the source does not support, or null, one schema both asking and reading it, so an answer without that judgement is another shape (generation-12); a note is answered as `unsupported`, with whatever objectives remain, even none, still a 200, and null or blank is left out. Nothing is stored. `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/pdf-to-tutor.test.ts`
- **generation-4:** A draft keeps only what traces to the source: a quote located exactly once in it, a task valid by the task endpoint's rules with at least one located quote, and an objective with a valid title, at least one located quote, and at least one kept task. Everything else is refused by name and reason, such as "Objective “Greetings”, task “¿Hola?”: the quote “Hola, amigo.” does not occur in the source.": the owner reviews only what was kept, so the model's positions would point nowhere. The objective's title, the task's question, and the quote's words each have their whitespace collapsed and are cut to 80 code points. A hand-written task may cite nothing; a drafted one may not. `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`
- **generation-5:** A draft holds at most twelve objectives and five tasks each, and only the first three quotes of a task and five of an objective are looked for; whatever is past a cap is named in `refused`. So one draft, accepted whole, fits the authoring endpoints' 200 quotes per request. `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`
- **generation-6:** A source over 200,000 characters is a 400 saying to add it as several sources, a chapter each. The audience, the owner's description of the learners, is optional and at most 200 characters, else a 400. The goal, what learners should be able to do, is optional in the request; present, it must be storable text of 1 to 300 characters once trimmed, else a 400 naming it. The model is told a default audience, young learners at school, only when neither is given. The language for learners' instructions, the language of objectives' titles and of tasks' prompts and explanations, is optional in the request; present, it must be a BCP 47 tag checked as a source's language is (sources-3), of any language rather than only those Braivo's apps speak, else a 400 naming it; absent, the model is told to use the learners' language. The model is asked for it and told the material's words being taught stay as they are: asked, not checked. `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`
- **generation-7:** Each drafted objective's key is random, made with the draft: accepting the same draft again finds the objectives it already made, and another draft of the same source makes new ones. `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/pdf-to-tutor.test.ts`
- **generation-8:** Only an `owner`, `admin`, or `author` of the organization may read a file or draft with the installation's AI, under its gate and quota, which an author does not manage ([ADR 0048](../adr/0048-author-role.md)); anyone else gets a bare 403, before anything about the installation's model, the organization's entitlement, or its sources is revealed. `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/roles.test.ts`
- **generation-9:** Without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, both requests answer 501 with `code` `AI_UNAVAILABLE` and an `error` pointing to one's own AI app connected to Braivo; reading a file where the installation keeps no files answers generation-2's uncoded 501 first. With a key, every organization may use it unless `BRAIVO_AI_ORGANIZATIONS` lists the IDs that may; any other answers 403 with `code` `AI_NOT_ENTITLED` and an `error` saying to ask the operator. That variable set but naming no ID, or `*`, stops the server at startup, as does a key without it on an installation with `BRAIVO_SELF_SERVE_DOMAIN` (access-31). `BRAIVO_AI_MODEL` picks the model, `claude-sonnet-5` by default. `apps/server/config.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`
- **generation-10:** Every request that reaches the model is recorded for its organization: what was asked (`read` or `draft`), by whom, when, the model (`BRAIVO_AI_MODEL`'s name for it), and the version of Braivo's prompt, a fingerprint of everything Braivo tells the model for that kind of request but never what a request fills in (the goal, the audience, the language for instructions, the material, the file). A request refused as invalid, unknown, or because the stored file could not be fetched is not recorded; one the model then fails still is. Recorded before the model is asked, it is then completed with how it went: `succeeded`, with a draft's objectives and tasks proposed and kept (passing Braivo's checks, not accepted by the owner) and its refusals counted by kind (one refused task may count under two kinds, so they do not sum to what was dropped), or a reading's pages and the blank pages it dropped; `failed`, with a kind (`unreachable`, `timeout`, `provider-error`, `cut-short`, `wrong-shape`, `invalid-output`, `no-words`, or `error` for Braivo's own); or `aborted` when the caller went away. A request whose completion was never written, because the process ended first or the write failed, stays `unknown`; that write never changes the answer, nor delays it by more than two seconds. No words of the material, the draft, or an error are recorded. `apps/server/application/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/persistence/ai-request.test.ts`, `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`
- **generation-11:** With `BRAIVO_AI_MONTHLY_LIMIT` set, a whole number of at least 1, an organization that has made that many requests in the calendar month (UTC), however each went, is answered 429, with `Retry-After` until the first of next month and an `error` naming that date and one's own AI app meanwhile. Two requests racing for the last one cannot both have it. Unset, requests are recorded and never capped. `apps/server/persistence/ai-request.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/server/config.test.ts`
- **generation-12:** A model that cannot be reached, does not answer within its five minutes, answers an error, is cut short, or answers in another shape, or a file in which it finds no words, is a 502 with an `error` saying which; nothing is stored. `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`
- **generation-13:** Reading and drafting wait for the model past the server's ten-second idle timeout, bounded by the model's own five-minute one. A caller that goes away stops Braivo waiting and asks the provider to stop; the request still counts (generation-10). While a draft or a reading runs, the console says it can take a few minutes; it aborts a draft, an upload, and a reading when the owner leaves the page, and when a proxy in front of Braivo cuts a draft or a reading off (504, or Cloudflare's 524, without Braivo's reason) it says so, that the request may have counted, and to send less material or have the proxy wait longer, rather than to try again. `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/server/ai/ai.test.ts`, `apps/console/test/sources.test.tsx`, `apps/console/test/drafting.test.tsx`
- **generation-14:** In the console, adding material with a photo and no pasted text, or a PDF the owner chooses to have the AI read, has Braivo read the file, where its AI is on (generation-19), and adds its pages as a source with the file as its original; a PDF is otherwise read from its own text, without AI ([sources](sources.md), sources-22), and the AI's reading of one asked for from its review replaces that review, for the owner to save (sources-23). A file outside the types and sizes Braivo reads (generation-2) is refused before it is uploaded, saying what to do instead; with text pasted, a file the upload takes is kept as the original, unread. A refused addition sent again does not read the same file twice. `apps/console/test/sources.test.tsx`
- **generation-15:** The console asks for the goal before drafting, the audience optionally, and the language for learners' instructions, by name or tag, starting from the language the console itself speaks, and reviews a draft on its source's page, and only there: it says what it was asked for, as sent, the model's `unsupported` note right under that, named as the AI's; a draft with no objectives says that nothing in it can become a course, and why only as far as known: the AI's note, Braivo having left out all it proposed, or no reason given, then that discarding it, the only action offered, lets the owner draft again for another AI request; what Braivo refused is listed, every objective and task is kept until unticked, dropping an objective drops its tasks, and nothing is stored until the owner creates the course. Leaving for another page while a draft is under review asks first, and so does reloading or closing the tab where the browser allows; once creating the course has failed, the question and the review say that some or all of it may already be stored, which neither leaving nor discarding undoes. The course title it suggests is the source's, for the owner to change: a goal describes what learners do, rarely what a course is called, and the review shows it above. `apps/console/test/drafting.test.tsx`
- **generation-16:** In review, an objective's title can be corrected, and a task's question and explanation, a choice task's options and correct option, and a typed task's accepted answers, one a line (its answer language is kept); a task's number of options cannot be changed, and neither an objective's passages nor a task's can be edited, since Braivo located them in the source. A task's edit is checked as the server would (blank, repeated, too long, too many accepted answers, not text) before it is saved, and the course cannot be created while an edit is open. `packages/ui/compositions/task-editor.test.tsx`, `apps/console/test/drafting.test.tsx`
- **generation-17:** In review, creating the course checks its title and its kept objectives' titles first (present, at most 500 characters, text), and that each kept objective keeps a task, since learners would wait on one without any (learner-loop-5); it sends nothing until they pass, saying what is wrong under the first field to fix and focusing it. Once sent, the reviewed fields are locked: trying again after a failure sends exactly what was first sent, so a retry that succeeds finishes that course rather than duplicating it, and Braivo's reason for a refusal is shown. Creating gives up when its four requests in a row, under one deadline, take longer than 10 seconds each plus the time what is sent takes at 100 KB/s, and a sign-out meanwhile keeps the review, offering sign-in in a new tab before trying again. Discarding remains possible after a failed attempt. `apps/console/test/drafting.test.tsx`, `apps/server/api/client.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/pdf-to-tutor.test.ts`
- **generation-18:** Accepting a draft is authoring through the ordinary endpoints, as the person accepting: objectives by their keys, then citations, then tasks, then a course keyed by the source, its title, and its objectives' keys. The same accepted draft again resolves to the same course, and keeping a different set of objectives makes another; keeping different tasks under the same objectives resolves to the same course, those objectives keeping every task added to them. A course accepted from a draft is ordinary authored content, and the learner loop does not know AI wrote it. `apps/server/api/pdf-to-tutor.test.ts`
- **generation-19:** Listing the organizations someone works in says, for each they author in, whether they may use the installation's AI: a model set and the organization let (generation-9), whatever its month's requests (generation-11); the entry of one where they only teach omits it (generation-8). Where it is off, the console says so before anything is prepared (on Courses with none yet, above Add material, and in place of a source's draft form), pointing to whoever runs Braivo or one's own AI app, such as Claude or ChatGPT, and refuses a file other than a PDF with no pasted text before uploading, asking for its text; a PDF it reads from its own text ([sources](sources.md), sources-22). Wherever the console offers one's own AI app, the offer links to the guide connecting one, in a new tab. A draft already asked for stays, under way or under review, and if it fails says why there. A refusal coded `AI_UNAVAILABLE` or `AI_NOT_ENTITLED` despite the listing is worded as its AI being off, never naming the operator's settings; any other, such as storage gone, keeps Braivo's words. `apps/server/application/permission.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/client.contract.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/materials.test.ts`, `apps/console/test/courses.test.tsx`, `apps/console/test/sources.test.tsx`, `apps/console/test/drafting.test.tsx`
- **generation-20:** In review, each drafted task kept says what a learner may run into with it as written, as the course page does ([authoring](authoring.md), Findings), from the draft as the owner left it; Edit and add “Hello” opens its editor with that answer added, changing only the draft, and the note goes once the task no longer has it. `apps/console/test/drafting.test.tsx`
- **generation-21:** The operator's `ai report` counts the recorded requests since a date (UTC; the current month's first by default), for one organization by its slug or for all, a line per kind, model, and prompt version: how many, how many succeeded, failed by kind, were aborted, or are `unknown`, and over succeeded drafts the objectives and tasks kept of those proposed and the three most frequent refusal kinds. Requests recorded before the model and prompt version were show them as not recorded. It reads no content. `apps/server/cli/index.test.ts`

## Boundaries

- What a source is, its pages, original file, immutability, and how a quote is located and cited: [sources](sources.md).
- Objectives, courses, tasks, their keys and idempotence, and the MCP tools an AI app drafts with: [authoring](authoring.md). Drafting with one's own AI app through `/mcp` is the alternative to this area, at the owner's own AI cost, and needs no key on the installation.
- Who holds `owner`, `admin`, or `author`, and sign-in: [access](access.md).
- Accepting a draft in the console publishes its course as it is created, the console being where a person reviews; a course or task a tool writes through the authoring API waits for publishing there ([ADR 0039](../adr/0039-published-in-the-console.md)).
- Not here yet: AI grading and feedback on a learner's answer ([learner loop](learner-loop.md) until it outgrows it), a vocabulary lesson of more than twelve words in one draft (each word is an objective), video and audio, which reach Braivo as captions.

## Decisions

- [ADR 0021](../adr/0021-citations.md): proposals are not stored; review happens where a draft is made.
- [ADR 0024](../adr/0024-idempotent-authoring.md): keyed writes, so accepting a draft again finishes it rather than duplicating it.
- [ADR 0029](../adr/0029-server-drafting.md): Braivo drafts on request with one model port, checks the draft against the source, and stores nothing.
- [ADR 0030](../adr/0030-server-extraction.md): Braivo reads a PDF or photo into pages, returned for the caller to add; one gate for every AI request.
- [ADR 0031](../adr/0031-ai-limits.md): each AI request is recorded, and an operator may cap an organization's month.
- [ADR 0048](../adr/0048-author-role.md): an `author` reads files and drafts under the organization's gate and quota, managing neither.
- [ADR 0055](../adr/0055-pdf-text-in-the-browser.md): the console reads a PDF's text layer without a model, the AI only when the owner chooses it.
- [ADR 0046](../adr/0046-material-in-steps.md), accepted, not built yet: extraction and drafting in the background, a PDF's text layer before any model, a draft's scope.
- [ADR 0047](../adr/0047-structured-items.md), accepted, not built yet: structured items converted by Braivo beside drafting.

## Gaps

| Gap                                                                                                                                        | Impact                                                                                                                                                       | Next step                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reading and drafting are synchronous, tens of seconds for a chapter                                                                        | A proxy with a shorter timeout in front of Braivo cuts them off, still counted against the quota; the owner waits on the page                                | Extractions and drafts as background jobs, a step at a time ([ADR 0046](../adr/0046-material-in-steps.md))                                                     |
| No stored draft                                                                                                                            | Leaving, once confirmed or where the browser cannot ask (generation-15), loses a review, and a new draft costs a request                                     | Stored with drafting as a job (ADR 0046)                                                                                                                       |
| A course does not record that AI drafted it, nor which model and prompt (generation-10 records the request, not what was accepted from it) | An owner or a school cannot tell, from a course, how it was made                                                                                             | Decided with stored drafts (ADR 0046)                                                                                                                          |
| A request cut off where the host ends the server's work with the response (a Worker) may stay `unknown` rather than `aborted`              | The ledger cannot tell such a disconnect from a process that ended                                                                                           | Complete the request within the host's request lifecycle                                                                                                       |
| The quota counts requests, not cost                                                                                                        | A 200,000-character chapter costs the operator many times a photo, under the same count                                                                      | Meter tokens where cost matters (Braivo Cloud does downstream); a per-organization limit later                                                                 |
| One limit for every organization, and only the operator's command shows the ledger                                                         | An operator cannot give one school more; an organization cannot see its own usage                                                                            | A per-organization setting and a console page, when a self-hoster needs them                                                                                   |
| A redraft's objectives are new, never matched to earlier ones                                                                              | Drafting the same source again and accepting it duplicates objectives already authored                                                                       | Match a redraft to existing objectives when teachers need it                                                                                                   |
| A transcription can misread a smudged scan or handwriting, skip a page, or lose a question's number or answer key                          | Quotes and tasks inherit the misreading; the owner sees it on the source's page (sources-20), or, for a PDF read from its review, before saving (sources-23) | An extraction the owner can correct before saving it as a source (ADR 0046); meanwhile add the material with pasted text                                       |
| A PDF over 24 MB, or longer than one answer holds, cannot be read                                                                          | A question bank of hundreds of pages is refused; a book is read a chapter at a time, by hand                                                                 | Read in the background, a batch of pages at a time (ADR 0046)                                                                                                  |
| A draft is of a whole source | A long book must be split into sources to be drafted; the owner cannot say which pages matter | A scope of the source (ADR 0046) |
| Drafting writes new tasks; it does not convert questions a source already holds                                                            | A question bank drafted is rewritten, partly, instead of kept with its answer keys                                                                           | A converter by the owner's agent ([authoring guide](../../apps/docs/content/guides/authoring.mdx)); Braivo's own ([ADR 0047](../adr/0047-structured-items.md)) |
| One provider, Anthropic                                                                                                                    | An operator with another provider or their own endpoint cannot turn on Braivo's AI                                                                           | Another small adapter behind the model port                                                                                                                    |
