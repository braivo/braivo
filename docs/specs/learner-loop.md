# Learner loop

Status: living; checked against the code on 2026-10-03.

A learner opens a course and Braivo keeps choosing what to practise next from what they know, struggle with, or may be forgetting; each answer is graded into evidence that shapes the next choice. This is [product.md](../product.md)'s core jobs 2 and 3, closed end to end without AI for `choice` and `typed` tasks. Integrators who grade elsewhere feed the same estimates through the evidence endpoint.

## Rules

- **learner-loop-1:** `GET /api/courses` and a course's `…/next`, `…/activity`, and `…/attempts` act for the session's user, on a learn domain its learner session's ([access](access.md)), and nothing in the request names a learner. `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-2:** `GET /api/courses` lists, by title, every published course ([ADR 0039](../adr/0039-published-in-the-console.md)) of every organization the learner is a member of, each with its number and label ([ADR 0044](../adr/0044-course-numbers.md)); on an organization's domain, that organization's alone ([white-label](white-label.md), white-label-4). With `?number=`, only the course so numbered there; on the installation's host, none; a number not written as one, from 1 to 2147483647 without a sign or leading zero, 400. `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/client.contract.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-3:** A missing course, an unpublished one, another organization's, one the host does not admit, one whose organization the learner is not a member of, and a task outside the course or waiting to be published all answer alike: 404. `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/next-objective.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-4:** Each read decides afresh: the learner's evidence at the course's organization up to `now` is replayed under the active model, and the next objective chosen from the course's in content order ([learning model](learning-model.md)). Evidence dated after `now` does not count. `GET …/next` answers that bare decision, for integrators with their own tasks, or 204 when the learner is caught up. `apps/server/application/next-objective.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/learner-in-course.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-5:** `GET …/activity` decides among the objectives with a task first, and among those without one only when none of those needs attention, answering that decision without a task (no activity). It answers 204, caught up, only when no objective needs attention, so no activity is never presented as caught up. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/learn/test/course.test.tsx`, `apps/learn/test/course-standing.test.tsx`
- **learner-loop-6:** For the decided objective, an activity offers the task this learner answered least recently: never answered first, then the oldest created, then by ID. A retired task, or one waiting to be published, is never offered ([authoring](authoring.md)); an objective whose tasks all wait has no task. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/tasks.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-7:** A task rests for a learner after Braivo accepts their attempt on it, and for nobody else: for ten minutes, or until three attempts of theirs at the organization on other objectives were recorded after it, whichever ends first. A resend, outside evidence, and another task of the same objective never count. Since the task offered is the least recently answered, a resting one means every task of the objective rests: the activity then selects again without that objective, unseen ones included, in course order. Only when every objective with a task that needs attention rests, ahead of any without a task, does it name the objective whose rest ends first and `retryAfter`, the seconds until it can be offered, rounded up. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-8:** An activity never carries the answer, the explanation, or the task's passages. `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/content/task.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-9:** Options are shuffled by learner, task, and that learner's last attempt time alone, unless the task keeps its authored order (`keepOrder`): a reload shows the same order, and each accepted attempt reseeds it. Each option carries its `choice`, and an answer names that `choice`, never a position. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/content/task.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-10:** An attempt names its ID, chosen by the client, its task, and its response: an option's `choice` to a choice task, the `text` typed to a typed one (at most 500 characters, stored as sent), or Show answer, `reveal: true`, to either, naming exactly one of the three. An ID outside 1 to 128 characters, a response that cannot answer the task, or a body that is not UTF-8 or holds a NUL or an unpaired surrogate anywhere, answers 400, recording nothing. The statuses in full, the write check's 403 and the body limit's 413 included, are in `apps/server/api/index.ts`. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/policy.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-11:** An attempt ID records, per learner and organization, at most one attempt and one evidence record, dated when it was first accepted. Resending it with the same task and response records nothing and answers the outcome recorded (learner-loop-13); the same ID with anything else answers 409. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/persistence/task.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-12:** A new attempt on a task resting for this learner (learner-loop-7, from its latest earlier attempt) answers 409, and one on a retired task 404, both recording nothing. A resend is neither: an attempt recorded before the rest began, or before the task was retired, still gets its grade. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/tasks.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/client.contract.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-13:** A learner never chooses an outcome: Braivo grades every attempt from the task, and answers `{ kind: "choice", outcome, correctChoice, explanation?, passages? }`, or for a typed task `{ kind: "typed", outcome, correctAnswer, explanation?, passages? }`, `correctAnswer` the first accepted answer. A typed answer succeeds when it equals an accepted one as compared (case, spacing, and typographic quote marks aside; accents and punctuation count); Show answer is a failure, its attempt keeping `reveal` ([ADR 0038](../adr/0038-typed-answers.md)). A resend answers the outcome its evidence holds, even where grading would now say otherwise; the rest is rebuilt from the task. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-14:** A grade carries the passages its task cites ([authoring](authoring.md)), as sources-17 shapes them, only when it cites any; an activity never does, since a passage could give the answer away. `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-15:** Only an `owner` or `admin` records evidence graded elsewhere about a member, themselves included, up to 1000 records a request, so a member who administers nothing never grades themselves. Each record names an objective of the organization, an `at` written exactly as `toISOString` writes it and at most five minutes ahead, and an ID of at most 256 characters, without a NUL or an unpaired surrogate, not starting `attempt:`, which attempts' evidence uses. Only a session may post it. `apps/server/application/record-evidence.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-16:** An evidence batch is stored whole or not at all. Resending a stored result is a no-op; a different result under an ID the organization already holds for the learner answers 409. It enters the same replay as attempts, but creates no attempt, so it neither rests a task nor reseeds a shuffle. `apps/server/persistence/evidence.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/record-evidence.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/client.contract.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-17:** A learner may study at several organizations at once: each records attempts and evidence only about its own tasks and objectives, under IDs of its own, and decides from its own evidence alone, so one organization's IDs never refuse or reveal another's. Reports stay apart too (progress-10). `apps/server/persistence/evidence.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/learner-progress.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-18:** `GET /api/courses`, `…/next`, and `…/activity` answer `Cache-Control: private, no-store`. `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-19:** The learn app lists the learner's courses, each a way into it, or says there are none yet; a failed load, or one unanswered within a read's deadline, offers to try again. A course's page is `/c/<ref>` ([ADR 0044](../adr/0044-course-numbers.md)): on an organization's domain the list links `/c/4732-everyday-english`, its number and its label, and elsewhere, or with the domain's organization unread, `/c/<course ID>`. A number, with any label or none, is found first (learner-loop-2) within a read's deadline; a label other than the course's is then replaced by it in the address, the history entry replaced, before anything else is asked, while a number alone stays as it is. One naming none of the learner's courses, or a ref that is neither, is not found, asking nothing else, which says to check the account signed in or ask whoever sent the link, beside the way back and Sign out. `apps/learn/test/courses.test.tsx`, `apps/learn/test/course.test.tsx`, `apps/server/content/course-ref.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-20:** A course page shows a way back to the list, the course's title, and where the learner stands (progress-8), leaving out the last two when they cannot be read, then one of the states below. It mints one attempt ID per activity shown and sends one attempt however many options are tapped, resending only that attempt. From sending it until the learner continues past it or it is refused, the tab keeps that attempt, with its activity, for that learner and course, and a visit (never a preload) resends it before anything else: graded, it shows as answered there; refused (400, 403, 404, 409, 413), it is forgotten and the course loads as usual; a 401 sends the learner to sign in, keeping it; with no answer from Braivo, it shows as unconfirmed. The app waits for an answer at most 20 seconds, for the activity and the session at most 10, and for the brand, the title, and the summary, read alongside the activity, at most 3, so a slow one holds back a question no longer than that: past that, the answer is unconfirmed, those three are left out, and any other read fails the page, which offers Try again. Continue, the page's Try again, a rest's end, and an answer refused with 401, 404, or 409 reload the course after rechecking the session (access-6), without reading the brand again. `apps/learn/test/course.test.tsx`, `apps/learn/test/course-attempts.test.tsx`, `apps/learn/test/course-standing.test.tsx`, `packages/ui/compositions/source-passage.test.tsx`
- **learner-loop-21:** Once a learner started `NEW_OBJECTIVES_PER_DAY`, twenty, objectives today at the organization, `…/next` and `…/activity` decide among the others alone; objectives started, acquiring or due, are never held back. An objective is started on the day of its first evidence at the organization, whichever course or outside grader it came from, and a day begins at 04:00 in the request's `timeZone` (an IANA name; UTC when none is named; 400 for an unknown one), so practice past midnight counts toward the evening's day. When objectives were held back and nothing else needs attention, both answer 200 `{ "dailyLimit": 20 }`, never 204 ([ADR 0041](../adr/0041-daily-limit.md)). `apps/server/application/daily-limit.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/learner-day.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/next-objective.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/client.test.ts`

## Boundaries

- Which organization a host reaches, and the course routes' host ceiling: [white-label](white-label.md), white-label-4. The evidence endpoint is an organization route, served on the installation's host alone.
- Who is a learner in which organization, sign-in, and the write check: [access](access.md). Membership stands in for enrollment.
- The language the learn app speaks, and how it writes times and counts: [localization](localization.md); the copy quoted here is its English.
- How objectives, courses, tasks, and their passages are made and retired: [authoring](authoring.md).
- How evidence becomes estimates and estimates a decision: [learning model](learning-model.md); where a learner stands: [progress](progress.md).
- Not here yet: AI grading, and a machine credential for the evidence endpoint (see Gaps).

## Decisions

- [ADR 0007](../adr/0007-one-learning-model.md): one active model; estimates are replayed, not stored.
- [ADR 0008](../adr/0008-courses-order-objectives.md): a course orders objectives, which is the candidate list.
- [ADR 0009](../adr/0009-evidence-read-whole.md): a decision replays the learner's evidence at the course's organization, never narrowed to the course.
- [ADR 0032](../adr/0032-learner-history.md): evidence and attempts record their organization, their IDs are unique within it, and a decision reads only its organization's.
- [ADR 0010](../adr/0010-hono-http-layer.md): the HTTP layer, and routes that call one use case.
- [ADR 0015](../adr/0015-tasks.md): immutable tasks, Braivo grades, client-named attempts, task availability as eligibility.
- [ADR 0017](../adr/0017-task-rest.md): the rest, ten minutes or three attempts on other objectives, selecting again rather than waiting, 409 rather than 429.
- [ADR 0041](../adr/0041-daily-limit.md): twenty new objectives a day, counted from evidence in the learner's time zone.
- [ADR 0021](../adr/0021-citations.md): a learner sees a task's passages in the grade, never before.
- [ADR 0018](../adr/0018-sign-in-and-invitations.md): organization membership stands in for course enrollment.
