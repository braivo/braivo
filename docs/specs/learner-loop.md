# Learner loop

Status: living; checked against the code on 2026-10-03.

A learner opens a course and Braivo keeps choosing what to practise next from what they know, struggle with, or may be forgetting; each answer is graded into evidence that shapes the next choice. This is [product.md](../product.md)'s core jobs 2 and 3, closed end to end without AI for `choice` tasks. Integrators who grade elsewhere feed the same estimates through the evidence endpoint.

## Rules

- **learner-loop-1:** `GET /api/courses` and a course's `…/next`, `…/activity`, and `…/attempts` act for the session's user, on a learn domain its learner session's ([access](access.md)), and nothing in the request names a learner. `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-2:** `GET /api/courses` lists, by title, every course of every organization the learner is a member of; on an organization's domain, that organization's alone ([white-label](white-label.md), white-label-4). `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/client.contract.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-3:** A missing course, another organization's, one the host does not admit, one whose organization the learner is not a member of, and a task outside the course all answer alike: 404. `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/next-objective.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-4:** Each read decides afresh: the learner's evidence at the course's organization up to `now` is replayed under the active model, and the next objective chosen from the course's in content order ([learning model](learning-model.md)). Evidence dated after `now` does not count. `GET …/next` answers that bare decision, for integrators with their own tasks, or 204 when the learner is caught up. `apps/server/application/next-objective.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/learner-in-course.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-5:** `GET …/activity` decides among the objectives with a task first, and among those without one only when none of those needs attention, answering that decision without a task (no activity). It answers 204, caught up, only when no objective needs attention, so no activity is never presented as caught up. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/learn/routes.test.tsx`
- **learner-loop-6:** For the decided objective, an activity offers the task this learner answered least recently: never answered first, then the oldest created, then by ID. A retired task is never offered ([authoring](authoring.md)). `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/tasks.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-7:** A task rests for a learner for ten minutes after Braivo accepts their attempt on it, and for nobody else. Since the task offered is the least recently answered, a resting one means every task of the objective rests: the activity then names the objective and `retryAfter`, the seconds until it can be offered, rounded up, and offers no other objective. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-8:** An activity never carries the answer, the explanation, or the task's passages. `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/content/task.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-9:** Options are shuffled by learner, task, and that learner's last attempt time alone, unless the task keeps its authored order (`keepOrder`): a reload shows the same order, and each accepted attempt reseeds it. Each option carries its `choice`, and an answer names that `choice`, never a position. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/content/task.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-10:** An attempt names its ID, chosen by the client, its task, and its response. An ID outside 1 to 128 characters, a response that cannot answer the task, or a body that is not UTF-8 or holds a NUL or an unpaired surrogate anywhere, answers 400, recording nothing. The statuses in full, the write check's 403 and the body limit's 413 included, are in `apps/server/api/index.ts`. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/policy.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-11:** An attempt ID records, per learner and organization, at most one attempt and one evidence record, dated when it was first accepted. Resending it with the same task and response records nothing and answers the same grade; the same ID with anything else answers 409. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-12:** A new attempt on a task resting for this learner (learner-loop-7) answers 409, and one on a retired task 404, both recording nothing. A resend is neither: an attempt recorded before the rest began, or before the task was retired, still gets its grade. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/tasks.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/client.contract.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-13:** A learner never chooses an outcome: Braivo grades every attempt from the task, and answers `{ outcome, correctChoice, explanation?, passages? }`, recomputed on every resend. `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-14:** A grade carries the passages its task cites ([authoring](authoring.md)), as sources-17 shapes them, only when it cites any; an activity never does, since a passage could give the answer away. `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-15:** Only an `owner` or `admin` records evidence graded elsewhere about a member, themselves included, up to 1000 records a request, so a member who administers nothing never grades themselves. Each record names an objective of the organization, an `at` written exactly as `toISOString` writes it and at most five minutes ahead, and an ID of at most 256 characters, without a NUL or an unpaired surrogate, not starting `attempt:`, which attempts' evidence uses. Only a session may post it. `apps/server/application/record-evidence.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-16:** An evidence batch is stored whole or not at all. Resending a stored result is a no-op; a different result under an ID the organization already holds for the learner answers 409. It enters the same replay as attempts, but creates no attempt, so it neither rests a task nor reseeds a shuffle. `apps/server/persistence/evidence.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/record-evidence.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/client.contract.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-17:** A learner may study at several organizations at once: each records attempts and evidence only about its own tasks and objectives, under IDs of its own, and decides from its own evidence alone, so one organization's IDs never refuse or reveal another's. Reports stay apart too (progress-10). `apps/server/persistence/evidence.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/activity.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/learner-progress.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-18:** `GET /api/courses`, `…/next`, and `…/activity` answer `Cache-Control: private, no-store`. `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **learner-loop-19:** The learn app lists the learner's courses, each a way into it, or says there are none yet; a failed load offers to try again. `apps/learn/routes.test.tsx`
- **learner-loop-20:** A course page shows a way back to the list, the course's title, and where the learner stands (progress-8), leaving out the last two when they cannot be read, then one of the states below. It mints one attempt ID per activity shown and sends one attempt however many options are tapped, resending only that attempt. From sending it until the learner continues past it or it is refused, the tab keeps that attempt, with its activity, for that learner and course, and a visit (never a preload) resends it before anything else: graded, it shows as answered there; refused (400, 403, 404, 409, 413), it is forgotten and the course loads as usual; a 401 sends the learner to sign in, keeping it; with no answer from Braivo, it shows as unconfirmed. The app waits for an answer at most 20 seconds, and for each read on the way to a question, the session's and the brand's included, at most 10: past that, the answer is unconfirmed, the brand, title, and summary are left out, and any other read fails the page, which offers Try again. `apps/learn/routes.test.tsx`, `packages/ui/compositions/source-passage.test.tsx`

| State                                               | Shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Next                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Task                                                | label ("New · Greetings"), why now, prompt, options; when the progress report lists objectives and none has started, a line on how practice goes                                                                                                                      | a choice or key 1–9 sends the attempt; options lock on it |
| Graded                                              | Correct, or Not quite with the correct option's text; any explanation; the correct option marked; and any passages under the heading "From your lessons", each naming its source and its moment or page, linked when the source has a link, a recording at its moment | Continue reloads the activity, with a new attempt ID      |
| Unconfirmed (network error, 5xx, no answer in 20 s) | "Your answer could not be confirmed."                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Send again, or a reload, resends the attempt and choice   |
| Refused (400, 403, 413)                             | "Something went wrong."                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | nothing; the same answer would be refused again           |
| 401, 404, 409 on an attempt                         | —                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | reloads: to sign-in, not found, or the rest               |
| Resting                                             | the objective and the local time practice resumes, rounded up to the minute                                                                                                                                                                                           | reloads itself after `retryAfter`                         |
| Caught up                                           | "You're caught up", nothing due right now, and when the next review falls due, if any (progress-8)                                                                                                                                                                    | nothing                                                   |
| No activity                                         | "Nothing to practise right now", naming the objective that comes next without a task                                                                                                                                                                                  | nothing                                                   |
| 404                                                 | "This course does not exist, or is not one of yours."                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | back to the course list                                   |
| Load failed                                         | "Something went wrong."                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Try again, or back to the course list                     |

## Boundaries

- Which organization a host reaches, and the course routes' host ceiling: [white-label](white-label.md), white-label-4. The evidence endpoint is an organization route, served on the installation's host alone.
- Who is a learner in which organization, sign-in, and the write check: [access](access.md). Membership stands in for enrollment.
- The language the learn app speaks, and how it writes times and counts: [localization](localization.md); the copy quoted here is its English.
- How objectives, courses, tasks, and their passages are made and retired: [authoring](authoring.md).
- How evidence becomes estimates and estimates a decision: [learning model](learning-model.md); where a learner stands: [progress](progress.md).
- Not here yet: task kinds other than `choice`, AI grading, and a machine credential for the evidence endpoint (see Gaps).

## Decisions

- [ADR 0007](../adr/0007-one-learning-model.md): one active model; estimates are replayed, not stored.
- [ADR 0008](../adr/0008-courses-order-objectives.md): a course orders objectives, which is the candidate list.
- [ADR 0009](../adr/0009-evidence-read-whole.md): a decision replays the learner's evidence at the course's organization, never narrowed to the course.
- [ADR 0032](../adr/0032-learner-history.md): evidence and attempts record their organization, their IDs are unique within it, and a decision reads only its organization's.
- [ADR 0010](../adr/0010-hono-http-layer.md): the HTTP layer, and routes that call one use case.
- [ADR 0015](../adr/0015-tasks.md): immutable tasks, Braivo grades, client-named attempts, task availability as eligibility.
- [ADR 0017](../adr/0017-task-rest.md): the ten-minute rest, waiting rather than switching objective, 409 rather than 429.
- [ADR 0021](../adr/0021-citations.md): a learner sees a task's passages in the grade, never before.
- [ADR 0018](../adr/0018-sign-in-and-invitations.md): organization membership stands in for course enrollment.

## Gaps

| Gap                                                                                                                                    | Impact                                                                                    | Next step                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/activity` drops objectives without a task before selection, so one `acquiring` from outside evidence no longer holds back later ones | Content order silently stops sequencing new material (untested)                           | Decide whether such an objective blocks or is skipped; pin it in `activity.test.ts` |
| Nothing alerts content owners that learners wait on an objective without a task; only its course page says so                          | Learners wait on it until an owner opens that course                                      | Alert owners, when a pilot asks                                                     |
| Two new attempts on one task at the same moment can both be recorded (ADR 0017)                                                        | Rest bypassable by racing requests (untested)                                             | Lock per learner and task if it is seen                                             |
| Attempts are not bound to a served activity: any course task outside its rest may be answered                                          | Fine for practice, not for assessment (ADR 0015)                                          | Server-issued binding when assessment is needed                                     |
| Every read replays the learner's whole history at the organization                                                                     | Cost grows without bound per learner (ADR 0009)                                           | Cache estimates once measured to matter                                             |
| The evidence endpoint accepts only a session                                                                                           | No server-to-server integration yet (`architecture.md`)                                   | ADR for a machine credential                                                        |
| Only `choice` tasks; feedback is right or wrong plus the author's optional explanation                                                 | No free-text answers, AI grading, or feedback on the learner's own work; core job 4 unmet | Next task kind per ADR 0015                                                         |

## Entry points

`apps/server/application/activity.ts` (activities and attempts), `apps/server/application/next-objective.ts` (the bare decision), `apps/server/application/record-evidence.ts` (evidence graded elsewhere), `apps/server/content/task.ts` (presenting, shuffling, and grading a task), `apps/server/persistence/task.ts` (choosing a task, storing an attempt), `apps/server/api/learning.ts` (the learner's routes), `apps/server/api/authoring.ts` (graded evidence's route), `apps/learn/routes/_signed-in/courses/$courseId.tsx` (the course page).
