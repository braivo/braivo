# Progress

Status: living; checked against the code on 2026-10-07.

For one learner in one course, Braivo reports where they stand on each objective: not started, learning, retained, or due for review, with how likely they are to recall it ([product.md](../product.md), core job 5). Administrators, and teachers for their groups, read it in the console, from a course overview that counts standings per member and per objective, the latter showing knowledge gaps across learners; learners read their own as a summary in the learn app. The report comes from the same replay that chooses the learner's next activity, so it explains selection rather than restating it ("Explainable decisions").

## Rules

- **progress-1:** A report may be read by the learner themselves, while the course is published and not hidden ([ADR 0039](../adr/0039-published-in-the-console.md)), by an `owner` or `admin` of the course's organization, or by a teacher of a group the learner learns in that takes the course ([ADR 0042](../adr/0042-groups-scope-teaching.md)), and only about a member of that organization; through a tool, another's report only where the organization allows it (access-45). Anyone else, other members, an `author` as such ([ADR 0048](../adr/0048-author-role.md)), and the teacher of another group or course included, gets the same answer as for a missing course. `apps/server/api/roles.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/learner-progress.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/groups.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/groups.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`
- **progress-2:** A refusal confirms nothing: a missing course answers like one the reader may not read, and a learner outside the organization like an ID nobody has. The reader is authorized before the learner is looked up, so a refused reader cannot tell a member from a stranger. `apps/server/application/learner-progress.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/learner-in-course.test.ts`
- **progress-3:** On an organization's own domain, only that organization's courses report; on an unknown host, none ([white-label](white-label.md), white-label-4). `apps/server/application/learner-in-course.test.ts`
- **progress-4:** The report lists every objective of the course, by title, in content order, those not started included; a course with no objectives reports an empty list. `apps/server/application/learner-progress.test.ts`
- **progress-15:** For a course with no objectives, the console's report of a learner says so in place of the standings. Otherwise, under a standing of retained or due for review, it shows the estimated recall now as a percentage, and says once that this is a model estimate, not a test score: just after a success, even one following a failure, it can read 100%, which does not establish long-term retention. `apps/console/test/progress.test.tsx`
- **progress-5:** What the report calls due is what selection would review: whatever selection chooses appears in the report as the reason. A retained objective also carries when it falls due, past or future: when selection's rule starts to hold. `apps/server/learning/assess.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/learner-progress.test.ts`
- **progress-14:** Each objective in a report carries the evidence its standing was replayed from, oldest first and ties as replay orders them: each result's outcome and time, under progress-6 and progress-10, and nothing else; an objective not started carries none. The console counts it by outcome per objective, so one failure and many read apart, and lists it dated, newest first, on request. A reader may ask for the report without it (`includeHistory=false`), as the learn app does, keeping the standings and their misses (progress-16); omitted or `true`, it is included, and any other or repeated value is refused. `apps/server/application/learner-progress.test.ts`, `apps/console/test/progress.test.tsx`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/learn/test/course-standing.test.tsx`
- **progress-16:** Each objective in a report carries its misses, when it has any: the failures, Show answer's included, recorded since its latest success, or since its first evidence without one, counted from the same evidence and in replay's order (progress-6, progress-10), with when the latest was and whether it was ever answered correctly. One still being learned with 3 or more reads as **repeated misses**: a signal to check on the learner, not a diagnosis, fixed for now and given in each answer (`repeatedMissesThreshold`) beside the moment it was assessed (`asOf`). Both come with or without the evidence. The console's report says it under the standing, "4 misses since the last correct answer" or "4 misses, no correct answer yet", with the latest's date; says when it was assessed, by date and time, never the time alone, which a page kept open past midnight would misdate; and gives each objective's heading an anchor, `#objective-<objectiveId>`, focused on arrival. The learn app shows none of it. `apps/server/learning/misses.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/learner-progress.test.ts`, `apps/console/test/progress.test.tsx`
- **progress-6:** A report describes one moment; evidence dated after it does not count. Nothing is stored: every read recomputes. `apps/server/application/learner-progress.test.ts`
- **progress-7:** Every progress answer, a report's or an overview's, refusals included, carries `Cache-Control: private, no-store`. `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`
- **progress-8:** A learner sees counts by standing that open into each objective by title; if their report cannot be read, the summary is left out and practice goes on. Once Braivo grades an answer given on the page, Show answer's included, the report is read again under the summary's deadline (learner-loop-20), so the counts agree with the feedback before Continue: meanwhile the earlier counts are hidden in place, and if it cannot be read the summary is left out, the feedback and Continue unaffected; a reload drops a read still on its way. When the report holds a retained objective not yet due, a caught-up learner is told which falls due next, and when, in their local time rounded up to the minute: today or tomorrow by the calendar, whose day begins at midnight since this names a clock time, else its full date. Where practice stops, caught up or at the day's limit, the learner is also told what they practised today: the course's objectives whose latest evidence, wherever it was recorded, falls today, a day beginning at 04:00 in the device's time zone as the daily limit's does, counted by their current standing; nothing when there are none or the report cannot be read. `apps/learn/test/course-standing.test.tsx`
- **progress-9:** The console never pairs one organization's course with another's learners: it reads nothing about a course until finding it in the organization, and shows any refusal as not found. An administrator's pages look for it in the organization's own listing; a teacher's, among the courses of the groups they teach there, and a learner's report, the learner among those groups' learners, never reading an administrator's listing. `apps/console/test/courses.test.tsx`, `apps/console/test/progress.test.tsx`
- **progress-10:** A learner who also studies at another organization is reported from this organization's objectives alone: evidence recorded there neither appears in the report nor changes it, and who may read either report is progress-1. `apps/server/application/learner-progress.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/course-progress.test.ts`
- **progress-11:** A course's overview lists, by name, every member of its organization to an `owner` or `admin`, with their roles, and to a teacher the learners of the groups they teach that take the course, each once, with their objectives counted by standing: not started, learning, retained, and due for review, adding up to the course's. Each count is what that learner's report shows at the same moment, and each learner links to their report, which names the course and links back to it. In the console it is the course's progress page, `/<organization>/courses/<course>/progress`, shared by administrators and teachers, which names its learners (a teacher's groups, or every member, staff included), then the course's learner link to send them, with Copy address, once the organization has a learn domain ([ADR 0044](../adr/0044-course-numbers.md)) and learners can open the course, published and not hidden, saying otherwise that they can't for now ([ADR 0039](../adr/0039-published-in-the-console.md)), and finds them by name (as typed, the URL following a moment later) and orders them by name, most objectives learning, or most due, kept in the URL and carried to a report and back; the objectives below count every learner, whatever is searched. `apps/server/application/course-progress.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/groups.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/authoring.test.ts`, `apps/console/test/progress.test.tsx`, `apps/console/test/groups.test.tsx`
- **progress-12:** Only an `owner` or `admin` of the course's organization, or a teacher of a group taking it, reads its overview, a group with no learners yet included, and through a tool only where the organization allows it (access-45); any other member, an `author` as such and the learner included, reads their own report alone. A refused reader and a missing course get one answer, as in progress-2. It is a console page, read on the installation's host alone: elsewhere it answers 401, as without a session. `apps/server/application/course-progress.test.ts`, `apps/server/application/groups.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/learning.test.ts`, `apps/server/api/groups.test.ts`
- **progress-13:** The overview also lists every objective of the course, by title, in content order, with its learners counted by the same four standings, adding up to the members; for each standing, summing the objective rows equals summing the learner rows. A course with no objectives lists none. In the console, each row of either list with anything to count also shows its counts as one bar under its name, coloured by standing as its column's heading shows ([ADR 0037](../adr/0037-console-theme-light-and-dark.md)); a screen reader reads the counts alone. `apps/server/application/course-progress.test.ts`, `apps/console/test/progress.test.tsx`

## Boundaries

- The estimates, phases, and what "due" means belong to the [learning model](learning-model.md); this area names and shows them.
- Who belongs to an organization, and so to its courses, and who teaches which group, is [access](access.md).
- Not here yet: history over time (see Gaps).

## Decisions

- [ADR 0007](../adr/0007-one-learning-model.md): one active model, so a report is recomputed rather than stored.
- [ADR 0009](../adr/0009-evidence-read-whole.md): a report replays the learner's evidence at the course's organization, never narrowed to the course.
- [ADR 0010](../adr/0010-hono-http-layer.md): the reader comes from the session, the learner from the path; the body is the use case's report, serialized.
- [ADR 0018](../adr/0018-sign-in-and-invitations.md): membership is enrollment, so every member appears in every course's overview an administrator reads.
- [ADR 0042](../adr/0042-groups-scope-teaching.md): a teacher reads their groups' learners in their groups' courses, from the same organization-wide evidence an administrator's view replays.
- [ADR 0048](../adr/0048-author-role.md): authoring grants no learner's progress; an author who teaches reads their groups' alone.
- [ADR 0032](../adr/0032-learner-history.md): one history per learner, recorded and read per organization.
- [ADR 0050](../adr/0050-tools-read-learner-data.md): an organization decides whether tools read others' progress, off by default.

## Gaps

| Gap                                                                                             | Impact                                                                                                                | Next step                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Learning" covers both one failure and many on the course overview                              | A stuck learner looks there like one who just started, until their own report is opened                               | Open question for the learning model: a failure or evidence count per standing                    |
| A report is always at the request's `now`                                                       | No trend or before/after view                                                                                         | Open question: accept an instant, which replay already supports                                   |
| No MCP tool reads progress: an AI app connected at `/mcp` has `braivo:author` alone (access-41) | Administrators and teachers read progress in the console, or with a token where their organization lets tools read it | `braivo:progress`, behind access-45's setting ([ADR 0045](../adr/0045-remote-mcp.md))             |
| An administrator's progress page lists every organization member, unpaged                       | Administrators count as learners in both tables, since anyone may be learning                                         | List course participants once enrollment or activity says                                         |
| A teacher's progress page combines every group they teach the course to                         | One class's objective counts cannot be told apart from another's                                                      | A group's overview: its learners and objectives counted for the same learners, at the same moment |

## Entry points

`apps/server/application/learner-progress.ts` (a learner's report), `apps/server/application/learner-in-course.ts` (load shared with selection), `apps/server/application/course-progress.ts` (a course's overview), `apps/server/application/access.ts` (whether a tool reads others' progress), `apps/server/api/learning.ts` (the report's and the overview's routes), `apps/console/routes/_signed-in/$organizationSlug/courses/$courseId/progress.tsx` (console overview), `apps/learn/routes/_signed-in/c/$courseRef.tsx` (learner summary), `apps/console/routes/_signed-in/$organizationSlug/courses/$courseId/learners/$learnerId.tsx` (console report).
