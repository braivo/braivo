2. Grammar / Gramatyka
Pytamy uprzejmie: Could I have…? oraz Which…? Przykłady:
Could I have a return ticket to Oxford, please?
Which platform does the next train leave from?
The next train is delayed.
3. Dialogue / Dialog
Clerk: Good morning. Where to?
Anna: Could I have a return ticket to Oxford, please?
Clerk: That's twelve pounds.
Anna: Which platform does the next train leave from?
Clerk: Platform four. But the next train is delayed.
Anna: Do I have to change trains?
Clerk: No, it's direct. Check the timetable on the platform.
4. Exercises / Ćwiczenia
a) Przetłumacz: bilet powrotny (answer: a return ticket)
b) Przetłumacz: peron (answer: the platform)
c) Uzupełnij: Could I have a ___ ticket to Oxford, please? (answer: return)
d) Przetłumacz: The next train is delayed. (answer: Następny pociąg jest opóźniony.)
e) Przetłumacz: przesiadać się (answer: to change trains)
f) Przetłumacz: a single ticket (answer: bilet w jedną stronę)
